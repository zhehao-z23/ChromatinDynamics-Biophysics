# Overall shared-frame Site1/Site2 separation

All 59,105 recorded frames with valid Site1 and Site2 coordinates were retained. No trajectory-level QC, motion, separation, 53BP1, hour, acquisition or learned-state filter was applied.

The median observed 2D separation is 336.2 nm. 20,584/59,105 shared frames (34.8%) have separation strictly greater than 550 nm. Frames are repeated observations, not independent biological replicates.

## Exploratory threshold sensitivity

A post-hoc 400--1000 nm scan was kept separate from the primary display. Among 50-nm-aligned thresholds satisfying strict increase from 1.5 through 4.5 h and both 5/10 h below 4.5 h, the visual candidate is 550 nm. It is not a confirmatory cutoff.
